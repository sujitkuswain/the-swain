Our Story

A Small Hotel.
A Genuine Welcome.

The Swain is a family-run hotel in the heart of Boudh. We built this place for families visiting for work, pilgrimage, or leisure — people who want a room that's clean, a meal that's proper, and staff who treat them like guests, not just bookings.

We are not a resort. We are a well-run hotel — and we take that seriously.

Location Town centre, Boudh — walkable to market & bus stand
